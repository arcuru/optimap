Goal: Understand how group width (16/32/64), overflow strategy (separate/embedded), tag strategy, indexing mode, overflow channels, and stride interact across N. Run benchmarks, combine best features, identify winners.

## Completed

1. **Ran full 27-design sweep** (N=100..2M entries, 5 ops, 3 trials) of all 32/64-slot separate, embedded, and AND-indexed variants
2. **Analyzed results**: 32-slot has narrow insert win (5-15% at 1M+), tied hit, 10% miss penalty. 64-slot is behind across the board. Top255 critical for wider groups.
3. **Extended sweep bench**: Added all 32/64-slot and embedded variants to `for_each_design!` macro in `benches/sweep.rs`
4. **Generated plots**: 4 gnuplot PNGs comparing 32/64-slot vs 16-slot across N-curve
5. **Documented findings**: New `docs/src/optimization/32-64-slot-investigation.md` chapter, updated roadmap
6. **Created scripts**: `scripts/sweep-32-64.sh`, `scripts/analyze-32-64.py`, `scripts/plot-32-64.gp` for re-running

## Key Conclusion

32/64-slot variants don't provide a net general-purpose win over 16-slot designs. The investigation is fully closed. Next: OptiMap tombstone accumulation at scale.
